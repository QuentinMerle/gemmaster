# 🌃 Cyberpunk Noir: Neo-Lyon 2099
The city is a vertical labyrinth of neon and chrome, perpetually soaked in acidic rain. Mega-corporations own the air, and hackers sell memories on the black market.
**Tone**: Gritty, fast-paced, cynical.
**Hazards**: Corporate strike teams, neural viruses, glitchy cybernetics.
**Key Locations**: Sector 4 Slums, The Zenith Spire, Under-Net Hubs.
