# 🏰 High Fantasy: The Crystal Keep
The world is a tapestry of ancient magic and fading empires. Floating islands orbit a dying sun, and crystalline resonances power the remnants of civilization.
**Tone**: Epic, lyrical, mysterious.
**Hazards**: Shifting geometry, resonance storms, corrupted guardians.
**Key Locations**: The Whispering Woods, The Glass Citadel, The Void Chasm.
